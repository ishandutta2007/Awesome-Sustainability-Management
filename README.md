# Awesome-Sustainability-Management

# Top Sustainability Management Platforms Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on ESG Reporting, Carbon Accounting, Climate Disclosure, EHS Integration & Sustainability Data Management*
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Sustainability Management / ESG**. These tools help organizations measure, manage, and report environmental, social, and governance performance — including greenhouse-gas inventories (Scopes 1/2/3), regulatory disclosures (CSRD, ISSB, etc.), and broader sustainability program management.

**Examples** include Sphera, Enablon, Cority, FigBytes, IBM Envizi, Intelex, Diligent ESG, Novisto, Persefoni, and Workiva (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Fully featured enterprise ESG suites are limited in pure open source, but strong foundations exist for carbon accounting, emissions tracking, and sustainability data management. This section is expanded with the most relevant open projects, libraries, and modules.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[Sphera](https://sphera.com/)**  
  Enterprise sustainability, EHS, and LCA platform with deep capabilities for industrial companies, product stewardship, and supply-chain environmental impact.

- **[Enablon](https://www.enablon.com/)**  
  Integrated EHS, sustainability, and risk management platform used by large organizations for operational and corporate sustainability data.

- **[Cority](https://www.cority.com/)**  
  EHS and sustainability cloud that connects operational environmental data with corporate ESG reporting and compliance workflows.

- **[FigBytes](https://figbytes.com/)**  
  Sustainability and ESG management platform focused on data collection, performance tracking, and disclosure readiness.

- **[IBM Envizi](https://www.ibm.com/products/envizi)**  
  ESG data and analytics suite (now under IBM) strong in consolidating energy, emissions, and sustainability metrics across multi-site enterprises.

- **[Intelex](https://www.intelex.com/)**  
  EHS and sustainability management system supporting environmental compliance, incident management, and sustainability performance tracking.

- **[Diligent ESG](https://www.diligent.com/)**  
  ESG and sustainability reporting capabilities within the broader Diligent governance platform, aimed at board-level and regulatory disclosure.

- **[Novisto](https://novisto.com/)**  
  ESG data management and reporting platform designed to streamline collection, calculation, and disclosure across frameworks.

- **[Persefoni](https://www.persefoni.com/)**  
  Climate management and carbon accounting platform emphasizing financial-grade emissions data, particularly strong for financed emissions and PCAF-aligned reporting.

- **[Workiva](https://www.workiva.com/)**  
  Connected reporting and compliance platform widely used for ESG and sustainability disclosures alongside financial reporting.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[Hyperledger Labs – Blockchain Carbon Accounting](https://github.com/hyperledger-labs/blockchain-carbon-accounting)**  
  Open project implementing blockchain applications for emissions calculations, carbon trading, climate claim validation, and related climate-action use cases.

- **[Odoo Sustainability Modules](https://github.com/sustainability-suite/sustainability-odoo)**  
  Suite of open Odoo modules for tracking CO₂-equivalent emissions, GHG Protocol support, CSRD-related features, emission factors, and sustainability accounting integrated with ERP processes.

- **[CodeCarbon](https://github.com/mlco2/codecarbon)**  
  Popular open-source Python library that tracks and estimates carbon emissions from computing workloads, useful for Scope 2/3 software and IT footprint measurement.

- **[Green Metrics Tool](https://github.com/green-coding-solutions/green-metrics-tool)**  
  Open tool for measuring energy consumption and carbon emissions of software, with timelines, comparisons, dashboards, and optimization insights.

- **[Open carbon accounting agents and toolkits](https://github.com/)**  
  Emerging open AI-agent and MCP-style toolkits designed for auditable Scope 1/2/3 carbon accounting and decarbonization workflows.

- **[pymrio and EEIO / LCA open libraries](https://github.com/)**  
  Python packages for multi-regional input-output analysis and life-cycle assessment that underpin many carbon-footprint calculations.

- **[CityCatalyst and city-level carbon accounting](https://github.com/)**  
  Open-source carbon accounting tools oriented toward cities and local governments, adaptable for organizational inventories.

- **[OS-Climate and sustainable-finance open projects](https://github.com/)**  
  Community efforts providing open data, models, and tools for climate risk, sustainable finance, and related analytics.

- **[Open emission-factor databases and converters](https://github.com/)**  
  Projects that curate, normalize, or serve emission factors for use in custom carbon-accounting engines.

- **[Sustainability dashboards and reporting templates](https://github.com/)**  
  Open BI and notebook templates for visualizing emissions, targets, and ESG KPIs using tools such as Superset, Metabase, or Jupyter.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Integration of carbon libraries with open ERP/accounting systems (Odoo, ERPNext, Beancount-style ledgers).
- Open data pipelines for collecting utility, travel, and procurement data that feed emissions calculations.
- Framework mapping tools that align collected data to GRI, ESRS, ISSB, TCFD, and other disclosure standards.
- Community-maintained lists of emission factors and activity-data sources.
- Blockchain and ledger experiments for transparent carbon credit and claim tracking.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine open emission-factor libraries and calculation engines (CodeCarbon, pymrio, custom GHG Protocol logic) with an open ERP or data platform (Odoo Sustainability modules or a data warehouse). Store activity data and calculated emissions in a controlled database, generate reports with open BI tools, and optionally use blockchain components for claim verification. This approach gives full ownership of methodology and data — suitable for organizations with internal sustainability and data expertise — while commercial platforms still dominate for complex multi-entity consolidation, assured disclosure workflows, and deep industrial LCA databases.

## Disclaimer
- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Sustainability and ESG reporting involve regulatory, financial, and reputational risk. Open-source tools provide transparency and methodological control but still require rigorous data governance, assurance readiness, and alignment with applicable standards (CSRD, ISSB, SEC climate rules, etc.). Always have calculations and disclosures reviewed by qualified professionals.
- Emission factors, boundaries, and methodologies must be documented and defensible.
